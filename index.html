Gastronomy & Word of Wisdom

Halal & Alcohol-Free Street Food Guide

Malaysia's rich culinary scene makes adhering to the Word of Wisdom effortless. Here are delicious, alcohol-free and tea/coffee-free highlights.

Jalan Alor Night Market

Bukit Bintang Hawker Hub
  • Charcoal Chicken Satay: Tender skewers marinated in lemongrass and turmeric, grilled over open coals and served with thick peanut sauce.
  • Roti Canai & Murtabak: Crispy, flaky pan-fried flatbreads served with dhal lentils and mild vegetable curry.
  • Grilled Chicken Wings (Wong Ah Wah): Legendary smoky glazed wings seasoned with soy and mild spices.
  • Fresh Young Coconut Water: Chilled whole green coconuts sliced fresh before your eyes.

Kopitiam Drinks (Word of Wisdom)

Alcohol, Tea & Coffee Free
  • Barley Ice (Air Barli): Slow-boiled pearled barley sweetened with rock sugar and pandan leaf; deeply cooling in the heat.
  • Milo Dinosaur: Iced chocolate-malt drink topped with a heaping mountain of un-dissolved Milo powder.
  • Fresh Sugarcane Juice: Stalks pressed through rollers with a squeeze of fresh calamansi lime.
  • Air Mata Kucing: Refreshing iced longan, winter melon, and luo han guo infusion.
